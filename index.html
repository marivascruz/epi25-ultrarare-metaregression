I. 100% Firth Rescue & Orthogonal WLS (§1–3)
Reconstructs finite effect sizes for 100% of coding variants via Firth 2×2 penalized likelihood and eliminates collinearity (median VIF = 1.019), expanding discovery across DEE, GGE, NAFE, and EPI to 21–22 unique genes at AF ≤ 0.0001 and 45 unique genes at AF ≤ 0.001 in M7.
II. Unbiased Length-Adjusted Pathways (§4A)
Exact Poisson-Binomial gene-length & variant-count adjusted enrichment across 21,568 gene sets resolves 8 core neurobiological systems: AIS voltage-gated Na+/K+/Ca2+ channels, GABAA/SNARE synapses, GATOR1–mTORC1 in NAFE, neuronal chromatin, and BBB transport.
III. Protein Size Calibration & <2,000 AA Core (§4B–4C)
Demonstrates that compact synaptic/chromatin genes (<2,000 AA, 97.5% of exome) explain 5.8× higher variance per gene (26.55% vs. 4.61%, e.g., STXBP1 63.6%, SLC6A1 60.8%), while adding back ≥2,000 AA unlocks pore-forming α1-subunits (SCN1A, CACNA1A/B, RYR2).

Unifies net coding location shifts (M8: rescuing GABRB3, CACNA1H, SLC25A11, PIEZO1, GRIN2D) and 3-d.f. folded-normal magnitude dispersion WLS (M9: rescuing SLC6A1 at AF ≤ 0.001, GTF2I, TRAPPC5), increasing discovery to 35 unique genes (AF ≤ 0.0001) and 70 unique genes (AF ≤ 0.001).

4D. Incorporating Risk & Protective Effects in Rare-Variant Meta-Regression: Model 2 (M8: 4-d.f. Joint Location + Slope) & Model 3 (M9: 3-d.f. Folded-Normal Magnitude Dispersion & Bidirectional Omnibus)

Standard 3-d.f. functional slope meta-regression (M7) tests only whether variant log-odds ratios covary monotonically with functional severity (H0: γAM = γLoF = γCon = 0) after centering out the gene-level intercept β0g. In human epilepsy, ion channels and receptors frequently harbor both net gene-wide coding shifts (Δβ0g ≠ 0, Zloc,g ≠ 0) and opposing gain-/loss-of-function vs. protective missense alleles across transmembrane vs. cytoplasmic domains. We extend the collinearity-free framework with Model 2 (M8) and Model 3 (M9) across DEE, GGE, NAFE, and EPI.

Model 2 (M8): Joint 4-d.f. Location + Slope Omnibus Meta-Regression

Calibrates each gene's non-synonymous inverse-variance mean against the cohort × Firth-fallback synonymous neutral baseline (μ̂0,syn) to form an orthogonal 1-d.f. net coding location shift Zloc,g (< 0 for net protective coding burden; > 0 for net risk coding burden), unified with the 3-d.f. within-gene moderator slope test F3,Mg−4M7:

F4, Mg−4M8 = ( Zloc,g² + 3 · F3, Mg−4M7 ) / 4 ∼ F(4, Mg − 4)
Newly Rescued Epilepsy Loci (pM8 < 3.0×10−7, Missed by M7 Alone):
• GABRB3 (GABAA Receptor β3 in DEE): Rescued at both ultra-rare AF ≤ 0.0001 (pM7 = 4.88×10−7 → pM8 = 9.15×10−8) and rare AF ≤ 0.001 (pM7 = 8.99×10−7 → pM8 = 1.56×10−7).
• CACNA1H (CaV3.2 T-Type Ca2+ Channel in GGE): Rescued across generalized epilepsy (pM7 = 4.19×10−7 → pM8 = 9.97×10−8).
• SLC25A11 (Mitochondrial Oxoglutarate Carrier in DEE): Rescued at AF ≤ 0.001 (pM7 = 7.18×10−7 → pM8 = 7.02×10−8).
• PIEZO1 & GRIN2D (Net Protective Shift Zloc < 0): PIEZO1 in DEE (Zloc = −4.45, pM8 = 9.11×10−9) and GRIN2D in GGE (Zloc = −4.38, pM8 = 2.05×10−7).

Model 3 (M9): 3-d.f. Folded-Normal Magnitude Dispersion & Bidirectional Omnibus

Model 3 (M9) directly regresses centered absolute variant effect magnitudes ugv = |β̂gv − β̄w,g| onto the 3 orthogonal functional moderators (AlphaMissense, pLoF, WES HMM constraint) via 3-d.f. Folded-Normal Weighted Least Squares (Fmag,g, pmag,g), unified with pM7,g via the equal-weight Cauchy combination test:

E[ |β̂gv − β̄w,g| | Xgv ] = α0g + αAM,g XAM + αLoF,g XLoF + αCon,g XCon  ⇒  pM9,g = ACAT(pM7,g, pmag,g)
Newly Rescued Bidirectional Loci in Epilepsy (pM9 < 3.0×10−7, Missed by M7 Alone):
• DEE (AF ≤ 0.001: 26 → 40 total genes across M7 ∪ M8 ∪ M9): Rescues SLC6A1 (GAT-1 GABA transporter at AF ≤ 0.001, pM7 = 6.53×10−7 → pmag = 2.79×10−8, pM9 = 3.84×10−8), GTF2I (pM9 = 1.20×10−7), TRAPPC5 (rescued at both AF ≤ 0.0001 and AF ≤ 0.001), and TAS2R20.
• Across All Epilepsy Syndromes: Unifying M7, M8, and M9 increases unique exome-wide significant epilepsy genes from 45 to 70 (+25 newly rescued) at AF ≤ 0.001, and from 21 to 35 (+14 newly rescued) at ultra-rare AF ≤ 0.0001.
